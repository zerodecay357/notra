Made for real lectures

Pause and resume freely, watch the live waveform, and capture tab audio for online classes. A crash costs seconds, not the lecture.

Private by design

Transcription is fully local. Only the finished text transcript is sent to Claude, with your own key — nothing else, nowhere else.

Notes worth revising

Not a wall of transcript — sections, boxed definitions, clean math, the questions asked in class, and a ranked exam-focus list.
